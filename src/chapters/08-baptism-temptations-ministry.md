<h2 id="voice-crying-in-the-wilderness">{% paraspan 0 %}{% sentence 0 %}Voice Crying in the Wilderness{% endsentence %}{% endparaspan %}</h2>

{% para 1 %}{% sentence 1 %}There is a man you need to meet before we go any further.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 2 %}A cousin to Jesus, John the Baptist.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 3 %}He was the son of a priest named Zacharias and his wife Elizabeth, who had prayed in their old age for a child until hope itself seemed fleeting.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 4 %}The blessing of conception was granted, but was only the beginning.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 5 %}While still within the womb of Elizabeth, John recognized the presence of the Messiah within the womb of Mary, and the babe leaped for joy.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 2 %}{% sentence 6 %}As an adult, John was not what you would expect.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 7 %}He wore clothing made of camel's hair, ate locusts and wild honey, and carried in his chest a burning knowledge that he had to share with anyone and everyone around him.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 8 %}Rather than teaching in the Temple or in synagogues, or even in the streets of cities, John lived and preached in the wilderness---not as a hermit hiding from the world---as a prophet to whom God had given a specific mission: prepare the way of the Lord!{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 3 %}{% sentence 9 %}The people of Judea went out to him in the desert margins by the hundreds---farmers, tax collectors, soldiers, Pharisees in their long robes.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 10 %}Something in his voice drew them forth.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 4 %}{% sentence 11 %}His message was not complicated: "Repent ye: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand." ({% scripture "Matthew 3:2" %}){% endsentence %} {% sentence 12 %}It was the message the prophets had always carried, compressed now to a single sentence, urgent in the way a man is urgent when he knows the hour has nearly come.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 5 %}{% sentence 13 %}Matthew paused to tell his readers who this was: "This is he that was spoken of by the prophet Esaias, saying, The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make his paths straight." ({% scripture "Matthew 3:3" %}){% endsentence %} {% sentence 14 %}Seven hundred years before John's birth, Isaiah had seen him.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 15 %}He had written him down.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 16 %}And here he was---exactly where the prophecy said he would be, calling Israel to the river, calling them to the threshold they had been approaching for four thousand years.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 6 %}{% sentence 17 %}I think about what it must have meant to stand in that crowd.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 18 %}To live under Roman occupation, to carry the long memory of a people who had been promised something they had nearly stopped believing would come---and to hear this man speak with that kind of certainty.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 19 %}Not the certainty of argument.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 20 %}The certainty of someone who had already seen what was coming and could not find words sufficient to prepare you for it.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 7 %}{% sentence 21 %}John himself said: "He that cometh after me is mightier than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear." ({% scripture "Matthew 3:11" %}){% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 8 %}{% sentence 22 %}He was pointing.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 23 %}That was all he was doing.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 24 %}He was pointing, and burning, and preparing the ground.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

<h2 id="entering-the-river">{% paraspan 9 %}{% sentence 25 %}Entering the River{% endsentence %}{% endparaspan %}</h2>

{% para 10 %}{% sentence 26 %}Around the age of thirty, Jesus left Galilee and walked south to the Jordan.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 11 %}{% sentence 27 %}He was not coming to John as a seeker comes to a teacher.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 28 %}He was coming to fulfill a commandment of His Father---not for His own sake but for ours.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 29 %}He had no sin requiring forgiveness, no old life needing to be washed away.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 30 %}He came because God had asked Him to, and because everything required of His children was required of Him also.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 31 %}He would not stand apart from us even at the water's edge.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 12 %}{% sentence 32 %}When John saw Him coming, something happened in that righteous man.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 33 %}He had been baptizing crowds all morning.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 34 %}Then he looked up---and he knew.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 35 %}The Spirit bore witness to him before a single word was spoken.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 36 %}He said, "I have need to be baptized of thee, and comest thou to me?" ({% scripture "Matthew 3:14" %}){% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 13 %}{% sentence 37 %}Jesus answered simply: "Suffer it to be so now: for thus it becometh us to fulfill all righteousness." ({% scripture "Matthew 3:15" %}){% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 14 %}{% sentence 38 %}There is a whole theology in that exchange, and I am not going to unfold it here.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 39 %}What I want you to see is the posture.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 40 %}The Son of God---the Creator of heaven and earth, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in mortal flesh---standing in a river in first-century Judea, submitting to the hands of a mortal man, because His Father asked Him to.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 41 %}In His economy, humility is never weakness.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 42 %}It is always the doorway to something larger.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 15 %}{% sentence 43 %}John baptized Him.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 16 %}{% sentence 44 %}"And Jesus, when he was baptized, went up straightway out of the water: and, lo, the heavens were opened unto him, and he saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove, and lighting upon him: and lo a voice from heaven, saying, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased." ({% scripture "Matthew 3:16-17" %}){% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 17 %}{% sentence 45 %}The Father spoke.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 46 %}I do not know what that sounded like---whether the crowd heard words or only felt something shift in the air, whether the sound was the kind that enters through the ears or the kind that enters through the chest.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 47 %}What I know is that something was declared that day over the water, in front of witnesses, for the record: This is my Son.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 48 %}I am pleased with Him.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 18 %}{% sentence 49 %}Thousands of years of waiting.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 50 %}Forty years of wandering in Egypt's shadow.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 51 %}Seventy years in Babylon.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 52 %}Centuries of silence after Malachi.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 53 %}And then a voice---not from a burning bush, not through a prophet's trembling mouth---but from the Father Himself, speaking directly into the world He had made, claiming the Son He had sent.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 19 %}{% sentence 54 %}He came.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 55 %}As promised, He came.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

<h2 id="strength-in-the-desert">{% paraspan 20 %}{% sentence 56 %}Strength in the Desert{% endsentence %}{% endparaspan %}</h2>

{% para 21 %}{% sentence 57 %}He did not go to Jerusalem.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 58 %}He did not begin to preach.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 59 %}The Spirit led Him immediately into the wilderness---and Satan was waiting.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 22 %}{% sentence 60 %}Forty days.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 61 %}Fasting.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 62 %}Alone.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 23 %}{% sentence 63 %}The three temptations that followed were not random assaults.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 64 %}They were a precise examination---each one aimed at the same nerve, each one asking the same question under a different disguise: Are you actually who you think you are?{% endsentence %} {% sentence 65 %}And if you are, why are you doing it this way?{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 24 %}{% sentence 66 %}The first came when His hunger had reached its sharpest edge.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 67 %}Satan pointed to the stones and said, in effect: You have the power.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 68 %}Use it.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 69 %}Feed yourself.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 70 %}Jesus answered with scripture: "Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God." ({% scripture "Matthew 4:4" %}){% endsentence %} {% sentence 71 %}He did not debate.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 72 %}He did not negotiate.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 73 %}He reached for the word of God the way a man reaches for it who knows what he believes---not as a weapon, but as a fact that will not move.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 25 %}{% sentence 74 %}The second came at the pinnacle of the temple.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 75 %}Satan, remarkably, also reached for scripture: Surely the angels will catch you.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 76 %}Jump.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 77 %}Show everyone what you are.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 78 %}Jesus answered: "Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God." ({% scripture "Matthew 4:7" %}){% endsentence %} {% sentence 79 %}There is something worth sitting with here.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 80 %}Satan knows the text.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 81 %}He can quote it.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 82 %}This was never a test of biblical knowledge.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 83 %}It was a test of whether Jesus would make His identity into a spectacle---whether He would perform Messiahship for an audience rather than fulfill it through sacrifice.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 84 %}He would not.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 26 %}{% sentence 85 %}The third came on a high mountain, where Jesus was shown all the kingdoms of the earth in a single vision.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 86 %}Bow down to me, Satan said, and I will give you all of it.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 87 %}This was the most unguarded offer of all---a way to win without the cross.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 88 %}The kingdoms were real.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 89 %}The shortcut was real.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 90 %}Jesus could have had everything He came to establish---dominion over every nation---without Gethsemane, without Golgotha, without any of what was coming.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 91 %}All He had to do was bow.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 92 %}His firm answer was, "Get thee hence, Satan." ({% scripture "Matthew 4:10" %}){% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 27 %}{% sentence 93 %}This was not a temptation because power is evil.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 94 %}Not because the nations were not worth having.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 95 %}But because He had not come for dominion.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 96 %}He had come for redemption---and redemption requires sacrifice, not a spectacle; a cross, not a shortcut.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 28 %}{% sentence 97 %}I have often thought about these temptations in my own life.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 98 %}Each one is aimed at something real---the body's need, the soul's longing to be seen and valued, the impatient desire for the outcome without the cost.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 99 %}He refused them all, not with arguments but with a settled certainty about who He was and why He had come.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 29 %}{% sentence 100 %}Luke added a detail that should be recognized: the devil "departed from him for a season." ({% scripture "Luke 4:13" %}){% endsentence %} {% sentence 101 %}Not forever.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 102 %}He would return---at Gethsemane, at the cross, everywhere in between, pressing at the same identity again and again.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 103 %}But the wilderness settled something that would not be moved.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 104 %}Jesus knew who He was and what He had come to do.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 105 %}He had been tested at the point of His deepest hunger, His deepest longing for an easier way---and He had not flinched.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 106 %}Angels came and ministered to Him after.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

<h2 id="the-ministry">{% paraspan 30 %}{% sentence 107 %}The Ministry{% endsentence %}{% endparaspan %}</h2>

{% para 31 %}{% sentence 108 %}He returned to Galilee in the power of the Spirit.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 109 %}Word of Him began to spread before His arrival anywhere.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 32 %}{% sentence 110 %}That phrase in Luke deserves a moment: in the power of the Spirit.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 111 %}This was not a man who had survived an ordeal and needed time to recover.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 112 %}This was a man who had come through fire clarified---who knew exactly what He was carrying, and it showed everywhere He went.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 113 %}"There went out a fame of him through all the region round about." ({% scripture "Luke 4:14" %}){% endsentence %} {% sentence 114 %}He taught in the synagogues.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 115 %}He was glorified of all.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 33 %}{% sentence 116 %}His message was the same John had carried, now spoken by the One John had been pointing toward: "The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand: repent ye, and believe the gospel." ({% scripture "Mark 1:15" %}){% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 34 %}{% sentence 117 %}He walked the shores of Galilee and found fishermen---not scholars, not priests, not the people anyone would have assembled for a movement of this magnitude.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 118 %}Simon and Andrew casting nets.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 119 %}James and John mending theirs in a boat with their father.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 120 %}He said, "Follow me"---and they came up out of the water and left everything behind them on the shore.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 121 %}They did not know precisely what they were following.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 122 %}But something in the way He said it made remaining impossible.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 35 %}{% sentence 123 %}What the ministry felt like from the outside was a stormfront---the kind that moves across a whole region and changes the quality of the air.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 124 %}Crowds gathered wherever He went, pressing in from every direction.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 125 %}People who had been carrying their conditions in silence for years---the sick, the frightened, the ones the religious establishment had quietly written off---kept finding themselves in front of Him.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 126 %}And He kept stopping.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 127 %}He kept seeing the person the crowd had turned into an obstacle.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 128 %}He reached toward what the law said was untouchable.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 129 %}He asked the names of people everyone else stepped around.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 36 %}{% sentence 130 %}Something was happening that was larger than any individual healing.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 131 %}The world was getting a prolonged look at what God is actually like---not God explained or argued for, but God moving through Galilean villages at the pace of a man who has time to stop.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

<h2 id="this-scripture-is-fulfilled">{% paraspan 37 %}{% sentence 132 %}This Scripture Is Fulfilled{% endsentence %}{% endparaspan %}</h2>

{% para 38 %}{% sentence 133 %}He went home to Nazareth.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 39 %}{% sentence 134 %}He had been away---months now, perhaps longer.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 135 %}Word had reached them: healings in Capernaum, crowds following Him everywhere, things happening that defied explanation.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 136 %}The people in that synagogue knew Him.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 137 %}They had known His father.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 138 %}They had watched Him grow up on those streets, learn that trade, walk to this building every Sabbath since He was old enough to walk at all.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 40 %}{% sentence 139 %}He stood up to read, as was the custom.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 140 %}The attendant handed Him the scroll of Isaiah.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 141 %}He unrolled it to a place that seemed to find itself under His hand, and He read:{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 41 %}{% sentence 142 %}"The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised, to preach the acceptable year of the Lord." ({% scripture "Luke 4:18-19" %}; {% scripture "Isaiah 61:1-2" %}){% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 42 %}{% sentence 143 %}He rolled up the scroll.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 144 %}He handed it back to the attendant.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 145 %}He sat down.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 43 %}{% sentence 146 %}"And the eyes of all them that were in the synagogue were fastened on him." ({% scripture "Luke 4:20" %}){% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 44 %}{% sentence 147 %}That is Luke's way of telling us the room had gone absolutely still.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 148 %}No one was clearing his throat.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 149 %}No one was shifting in his seat.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 150 %}Every person in that synagogue knew what passage had just been read.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 151 %}They had heard it their whole lives---the great servant poem of Isaiah, the anointed one who would come, the year of the Lord's favor, the liberation of the captive and the broken.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 152 %}It was among the most beloved texts in Jewish worship.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 153 %}And the man who had just read it was sitting in the teacher's seat, looking at them.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 45 %}{% sentence 154 %}Then He said: "This day is this scripture fulfilled in your hearing." ({% scripture "Luke 4:21" %}){% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 46 %}{% sentence 155 %}Not: this scripture describes what I am trying to do.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 156 %}Not: I hope to embody something of this spirit.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 157 %}Not a metaphor, not an aspiration, not a program.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 47 %}{% sentence 158 %}This day.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 159 %}This scripture.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 160 %}Fulfilled.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 161 %}In your hearing.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 48 %}{% sentence 162 %}The room where He had grown up.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 163 %}The people who had seen Him every Sabbath from the time He was old enough to walk through that door.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 164 %}The town that knew the smell of His father's workshop.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 165 %}And He was telling them---through them, telling everyone---that the ancient promise had a face, and they were looking at it.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 49 %}{% sentence 166 %}I want you to imagine standing in that room.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 167 %}In that silence.{% endsentence %} {% sentence 168 %}The question in the minds of everyone in that congregation is the same one that has been building through everything we have read together---the long human search across the centuries, the voice of one crying in the wilderness, the whole arc of preparation that has brought us to this moment.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 50 %}{% sentence 169 %}Who is this man?{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}

{% para 51 %}{% sentence 170 %}In the name of Jesus Christ, amen.{% endsentence %}{% endpara %}
