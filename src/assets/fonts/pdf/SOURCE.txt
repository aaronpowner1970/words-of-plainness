Noto Sans, Version 2.015 (unhinted TTF): Regular, Bold, Italic, Bold Italic.
Used only for the PDF download of the Reflections panel (js/wop-reflections-panel.js),
fetched only when a reader asks for a PDF. The site's display fonts are not affected.

Why: full polytonic Greek (all 233 code points of Greek Extended, U+1F00-1FFF),
Greek and Coptic, Cyrillic and Latin, so a word such as "Ἰησοῦς" survives in the PDF.
pdfmake's bundled Roboto has no polytonic Greek. (CODE-SITE-20, SITE step 30,
ruling SITE-JOURNAL-PDF-GREEK option 1.)
