# Session 13, phase 3: registry rows whose document is a catechism (R6-44 enumeration, for the author)

**Read only. No row other than BSR-LU-03 was moved.**

**Search space.**
- All 47 Branch Source Registry rows in workbook v2.25r4, across all eight branches, including Eastern Orthodox and the 3 retired rows.
- A row is listed when any of these holds:
  - **title:** the standard_title names a catechism;
  - **genre:** the row is typed CATECHETICAL, or its document is a question-and-answer teaching text;
  - **division:** its stored chunks carry catechism locators.
- The 29 rows not listed name no catechism in title, tier or chunk locators. They are the confessions, articles, creeds, liturgies,
  councils and statements of faith of the other rows.

**Evidence.**
- Instrument: the author-rulings file (R6-6 adoption rows as ratified in sessions 7–12), the workbook row fields and the chunk store.
- No web source was opened in this session. An act "on record" means recorded in those files, with the instrument named there.

**Items for the author (ranked by how likely a B1(b) move is):**
1. **AN-02, BCP 1662 Catechism.**
   - *For a move:* Canon A5 names the BCP as doctrine, and the Catechism is integral to the BCP (R6-35).
   - *Weakness:* A5 names the book, not the Catechism. Canon A5 was read through one instrument and one publisher.
2. **RC-01, Catechism of the Catholic Church.**
   - *For a move:* *Fidei Depositum* may call the Catechism a norm for teaching the faith.
   - *Weakness:* its text was never read in these sessions. B1(b) speaks of a *confessional subscription*, which the Catholic Church does not have.
3. **AN-04 and AN-05.**
   - *For a move:* both are catechisms of churches that subscribe to formularies.
   - *Weakness:* no act on record names either of them as a doctrinal standard.
