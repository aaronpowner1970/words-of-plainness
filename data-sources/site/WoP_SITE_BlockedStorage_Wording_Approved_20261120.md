Summary (the toggle): How to let this site keep your answers

Body:

Your browser is set to stop websites from saving anything on your device. You can allow it for this site alone, and you can turn it off again whenever you like. Your answers stay only on this device; they are never sent to us.

- **Chrome or Edge:** tap the icon at the left of the address bar, open Site settings, and allow this site to save data.
- **Safari (iPhone, iPad or Mac):** turn off “Block All Cookies” in Safari’s settings.
- **Firefox:** tap the shield or lock icon in the address bar and allow this site.
- **Opened from Facebook, Instagram or another app?** Choose “Open in browser,” because some apps’ built-in browsers can’t keep anything.

**Before you change the setting, copy or download what you’ve written.** Reloading the page after the change starts it fresh, and anything typed before the reload is lost.

Under each browser line: a link to that browser maker's own official help page on allowing site data/cookies.
