### A9-F14. s30: the rally point, closing span of Article 9 (declared term, not scored): RATIFIED by Aaron 31 Oct 2026, 14:48 ET

> The Article confesses only what Philippians 1:15-18, Mark 9:38-40 and John 17:20-23 confess: wherever Christ is preached, His disciples rejoice; Christ forbade His disciples to stop one who worked in His name though he followed not with them; and Christ prayed that all who believe on Him might be one. This is the floor: the disciple rejoices in every turning toward Christ, differences over the way to Him are no warrant for forbidding one another, and whatever turns disciples from Christ or toward contempt for one another has lost its bearing. It does not settle which way leads most surely to Him, how the scriptures are rightly read or by what authority, what a church may require of its own members, or whether and how the traditions should be united; each tradition fills those in its own house.
