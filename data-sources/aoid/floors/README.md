# AoID ratified floors

These are the ratified scope-note documents for AoID items: the written floor a span
is scored against. They are the authoritative text, under codex A4(d) as amended by
AOID2-FLOOR-DOCS-LOCATION (30 Sep 2026). The copies in the claude.ai project
"Main WoP Editorial Project" are the working drafts these came from.

A span's `panel_comment` in `src/_data/apparatusData.json` is transcribed from the
floor here, verbatim, when the span is created (codex A4(a) and A4(d)). The
floor-transfer check compares the two.

Do not edit a floor here to match a panel comment. A floor changes only by the
author's ruling, and the change is exported here from the ratified document.
