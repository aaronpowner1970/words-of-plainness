## G1. Apostolic witness beside the prophets (Article 4, item 18, unscored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026

Where it goes: A04 s1 now reads "the witnesses of prophets, mortal men and women." The item adds the apostles beside them, as one clause.

> The Article confesses only what Ephesians 2:20, Acts 1:21-22 and 1 John 1:1-3 confess: God's word comes to us through the witness of apostles as well as prophets, those Christ chose to testify of what they had seen and heard of Him, and His household is built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ Himself being the chief corner stone. This is the floor. It does not settle whether the apostolic office continues, how apostolic authority is handed on, or whether the canon is closed; each tradition fills those in its own house. The Restoration's fuller teaching (living apostles as special witnesses of Christ, D&C 107:23; Articles of Faith 1:6) is amplifying witness, not the Article's claim.

Container word: "apostles." Most readers will read it as the Twelve and Paul, whose witness Scripture records. A Latter-day Saint reader also hears the living Quorum. Catholic and Orthodox readers hear succession through the bishops. The floor tests only the witness of those Christ chose, not the office's continuance.

## G2. Ascension, the right hand, intercession (Article 5, item 5, unscored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with the Restoration anchors

Where it goes: A05 ends its account of Christ's life at "rose again from death in glory on the third day" (s14). The item carries the account one step further, as one sentence.

> The Article confesses only what Acts 1:9-11, Mark 16:19 and Hebrews 7:25 confess, with Romans 8:34 and 1 John 2:1: the risen Christ ascended into heaven, is seated at the right hand of God, and ever lives to make intercession for us as our advocate with the Father. This is the floor. It does not settle the manner of Christ's presence in the Lord's Supper, whether "the right hand" is a place or a figure of authority, or how His intercession works; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Why the first exclusion matters: whether Christ's ascended body is in one place (Reformed) or present wherever the Supper is kept (Lutheran and Catholic, each in its own way) divided the Reformation. The floor states the ascension and stops there.
Restoration column (ratified): Moroni 7:27-28 (Christ "hath ascended into heaven, and hath sat down on the right hand of God, to claim of the Father his rights of mercy"); D&C 45:3-5 (Christ as advocate pleading before the Father).

## G3. Gathering: prayer, scripture, song, strengthening one another (Article 8, item 6, unscored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with the Restoration anchors

Where it goes: Article 8 speaks of fellowship ("We will sit at the table with every soul who has found Christ") but never of disciples gathering. The item adds one sentence, which is where the Plan suggested "We pray always" could go.

> The Article confesses only what Hebrews 10:24-25, Acts 2:42 and Colossians 3:16 confess: disciples do not forsake assembling together, but gather to pray, to hear and read the scriptures, to sing psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, and to exhort and strengthen one another. This is the floor. It does not settle the day of gathering, the form of worship, who presides, or the sacraments or ordinances kept there; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Why the first exclusion matters: the Adventist family keeps the seventh-day Sabbath and most others keep the Lord's Day. Naming either fails the other. Sabbath observance stays in Volume 2 (Deferred Items Register, section 6).
Restoration column (ratified): Moroni 6:4-6 ("they did meet together oft... to pray... and to speak one with another concerning the welfare of their souls"); D&C 59:9-12.

## G4. "Our Father" (Article 8, item 17, scored; moved here from Article 11 by the architecture map): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with 3 Nephi 13:9-13

> The Article confesses only what Matthew 6:9-13 and Luke 11:1-4 confess: Christ taught His disciples to pray to God as "Our Father," hallowing His name and seeking His kingdom and His will. This is the floor. It does not settle whether the prayer is said as written or taken as a pattern, which ending or doxology is used, or its place in liturgy; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Restoration column (ratified): 3 Nephi 13:9-13 (the prayer as taught at Bountiful).

## G5. Resist the adversary (Article 9, item 21, unscored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with the Restoration anchors

Where it goes: beside A09 s10, "warning us away from what would destroy us."

> The Article confesses only what James 4:7, 1 Peter 5:8-9 and Ephesians 6:10-11 confess: the disciple submits to God and resists the devil, standing steadfast in the faith and putting on the whole armour of God. ## G6. The Lord's Supper beside baptism (Article 11, item 16, scored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with the Restoration anchors

Where it goes: A11 s9, "Christ chose to be baptized to fulfill all righteousness, and instituted ordinances to remember Him always," names no ordinance but baptism. The item names the Supper.

> The Article confesses only what Luke 22:19-20 and 1 Corinthians 11:23-26 confess: on the night He was betrayed, Christ took bread and the cup and said, "this do in remembrance of me," and as often as His disciples eat and drink it they show the Lord's death till He come. This is the floor. It does not settle the manner of Christ's presence, whether it is a sacrament or an ordinance, who may preside or partake, how often it is kept, or which elements are used; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Why "which elements" is excluded: Latter-day Saints use water (D&C 27:2), and most others use wine or grape juice.
Scoring risk to flag, not a floor change: the Salvation Army, in the Wesleyan family, does not keep the Supper as a practice, while teaching that Christ instituted it. Under C4 one such member church can remove a family. The floor is therefore written as what Christ did and said, not as "disciples keep it." Whether the Salvation Army counts at that floor is for the scorer, with the Quakers checked in whichever family they are registered.
Restoration column (ratified): 3 Nephi 18:1-11 (Christ institutes the sacrament at Bountiful); Moroni 4-5 (the sacrament prayers); D&C 20:75-79.

## G7. Marriage held in honor (Article 11, item 11, unscored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with D&C 49:15

Where it goes: A11 s5, "Live it — in the body, in the home, in the world," is where the Plan anchored marriage and family.

> The Article confesses only what Hebrews 13:4 and Genesis 2:24 with Matthew 19:6 confess: marriage is to be held in honor among all, and what God has joined together, let no man put asunder. This is the floor. It does not settle who may marry (item 29b), the boundary of sexual relations (item 29a), whether marriage is a sacrament, divorce and remarriage, or marriage beyond death; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Why this floor is separate from 29a and 29b: it is the part of the marriage teaching every family holds whatever decision 3 rules. If decision 3 admits sexual holiness, G7 is where it joins. If not, G7 still stands alone. It shares Hebrews 13:4 with 29a. If both enter, the apparatus marks the pairing.
Restoration column (ratified): D&C 49:15 ("marriage is ordained of God"); "The Family: A Proclamation to the World" stays Volume 2 witness, as ruled for 29b.

## G8. Completing Acts 2:38 (Article 11): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with Articles of Faith 1:4

Where it goes: A11 s13 quotes only the first half of Acts 2:38: "Repent and be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins." The completion adds the verse's own ending: "and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost." The span stays a direct quotation, so the span's type does not change.

> The Article confesses only what Acts 2:38 confesses: those who repent and are baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost. This is the floor. It does not settle how or when the gift is given (at baptism, by the laying on of hands, or at conversion), who may administer it, or whether baptism is necessary to salvation; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Why this completion matters: it ties Article 11 to Article 9's Spirit roles (R2, the Comforter promised to disciples). Without it, Article 11 describes entering covenant and never says what God gives in return.
Restoration column (ratified): Articles of Faith 1:4 (the gift of the Holy Ghost by the laying on of hands); Acts 8:14-17 is biblical and stays in the biblical column as the laying-on-of-hands witness.

**Amended by Aaron 2 Oct 2026 (COWORK-AOID2-06a):** the exclusions add "or how baptism is related to the remission of sins", for Baptist and evangelical readers of "for the remission of sins". G8 as amended:

> The Article confesses only what Acts 2:38 confesses: those who repent and are baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost. ## G9. Fasting (Article 11, item 24, optional, unscored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with the Restoration anchors

> The Article confesses only what Matthew 6:16-18 and Acts 13:2-3 confess: Christ taught His disciples how to fast ("when ye fast"), not as a show before others but in secret before the Father, and the early Church fasted and prayed as it sought the Lord's direction. This is the floor. It does not settle how often, when or how disciples fast, whether fasting is required or a free devotion, or what is given with it; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Restoration column (ratified): D&C 59:13-14 (fasting and prayer, "rejoicing and prayer"); Alma 17:3 (the sons of Mosiah "had given themselves to much prayer, and fasting").

## G10. Christ will come again (Article 12, item 1, scored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with the Restoration anchors

Where it goes: Article 12 moves from resurrection (s1-s4) to judgment (s5-s6) without naming Christ's return. The item fits between s4 and s5: He comes again, and every soul is raised to stand before God.

> The Article confesses only what Acts 1:11, Matthew 24:30 and Titus 2:13 confess: the same Jesus who ascended into heaven will come again in glory, and His disciples wait for Him in hope. This is the floor. It does not settle when He comes, the order of events around His coming, a millennium, or whether His coming is preceded by an unseen presence; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Why the last exclusion is there: Jehovah's Witnesses teach an invisible presence of Christ since 1914 before a future coming in judgment. The floor tests the future coming, which they also confess. The millennial views (pre-, post-, a-) are the other fault line.
Restoration column (ratified): D&C 45:44 ("then shall they look for me, and, behold, I will come"); Articles of Faith 1:10 ("Christ will reign personally upon the earth"). The Volume 2 register already assigns the sequence and millennium to Volume 2 (section 6).

## G11. New heavens and a new earth (Article 12, item 7, unscored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, floor held at the promise, with the Restoration anchors as Volume 2 witness

> The Article confesses only what Revelation 21:1-5 and 2 Peter 3:13 confess: God will make all things new, a new heaven and a new earth wherein dwelleth righteousness, where He will wipe away all tears. This is the floor. It does not settle whether the earth is renewed or replaced, its timing, or who dwells where; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Tension to check with the existing text: A12 s7 says "What form that accounting will take, and what glories await, we leave to God." A sentence describing the new heaven and earth risks answering what s7 leaves to God. The floor above stays at the promise ("all things new," "no more tears") and describes nothing further. The drafted sentence must do the same, or s7 is contradicted.
Restoration column (ratified): Articles of Faith 1:10 ("the earth will be renewed and receive its paradisiacal glory"); D&C 88:17-20 (the earth sanctified and made celestial). Both are Volume 2 witness beyond the promise itself.

## G12. Make disciples of all nations (Article 13, item 20, unscored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with the Restoration anchors

Where it goes: beside A13 s3, "His invitation is to every tribe, in every tongue, and across every tradition." The commission is where that invitation comes from.

> The Article confesses only what Matthew 28:18-20 and Acts 1:8 confess: the risen Christ sent His disciples to all nations, to make disciples, baptizing them and teaching them to observe all that He commanded, and to be His witnesses unto the uttermost part of the earth; and He is with them always. This is the floor. It does not settle how mission is carried out, whether it is directed toward members of other Christian traditions, or who may baptize; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Tension to check with the existing text: A13 s9-s10 welcomes those of other creeds and commits to "careful restraint and Christ-like patience regarding things that might divide us." The Orthodox and some others object to proselytism among Christians. The exclusion keeps the floor on the commission and off the method, so the drafted sentence can say the commission and still honor s10: the witness is offered, never forced.
Restoration column (ratified): Mosiah 18:9 ("stand as witnesses of God at all times and in all things, and in all places"); D&C 88:81 ("it becometh every man who hath been warned to warn his neighbor").
