## One question governs all six floors: is the Spirit's personhood inside the floor?

Five of the six roles are spoken of personally in scripture: He teaches, He testifies, He comforts. Jehovah's Witnesses teach that the holy spirit is God's active force, not a person. If a floor says the Spirit acts as a person, that family does not count on any of the six. Every role would then pass at 12 of 13 at best, with the C4 no-position gap (Q-C4-NOPOS) still unruled. RULED 22 Sep 2026, AOID2-SPIRIT-PERSONHOOD, option 1, filed topic-only: personal language in the text is a container word. The floor tests the role, not personhood, and each tradition fills that in its own house. The floors below are written to that ruling.

## The existing Article 9 already names a Spirit, and it is a different gift

s10 reads "the Spirit of God is given to each honest conscience as a compass within." Its reach is universal ("each honest conscience"). In the manuscript's own terms that is nearer the Light of Christ, and in other traditions nearer conscience or prevenient grace. The roles below belong to the Holy Spirit as given to disciples: the Comforter promised to those who follow Christ (John 14:16-17). The new paragraph must not read as saying the same thing twice, or as merging the two gifts. **Drafting constraint, not a scope-note question:** the new paragraph names the Holy Spirit as promised to disciples, and it cross-references s10 rather than restating it.

## R1. God's presence: RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026

> The Article confesses only what Psalm 139:7 and 1 Corinthians 3:16 confess: by His Spirit, God is present with His people and is nowhere absent. This is the floor. It does not settle whether the Spirit is a distinct divine person, His relation to the Father and the Son, or how He proceeds; each tradition fills that in its own house. The Restoration's fuller teaching (the Holy Ghost as a personage of spirit, D&C 130:22) is Volume 2 witness.

Not imported: omnipresence as a metaphysical attribute. The floor is God's presence with His people, not a doctrine of divine substance.

## R2. The Comforter (item 12, previously scored ~97%): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026

> The Article confesses only what John 14:16-17, 26 confess: Christ promised His disciples another Comforter, the Spirit of truth, sent from the Father to abide with them. This is the floor. The one Greek title *paraklētos* is rendered Comforter, Counselor, Advocate and Helper; the Article names it once, and the apparatus carries the range. It does not settle procession (the *filioque*), personhood, or the manner of the Spirit's indwelling.

Drafting note: Comforter, Advocate and Counselor are one title. Listing them as three roles would present one word as three (architecture map, Section 4).

## R3. Teacher: RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026 (with Moroni 10:4-5 added to the Restoration column)

> The Article confesses only what John 14:26 and 1 Corinthians 2:12-13 confess: the Spirit teaches disciples and brings to remembrance what Christ taught. This is the floor. It does not settle whether that teaching comes by private inspiration, through the Church's ministry, or both; each tradition fills that in its own house. The Restoration's fuller teaching, that "by the power of the Holy Ghost ye may know the truth of all things" (Moroni 10:4-5), is amplifying witness, not the Article's claim; the apparatus links it to Volume 2's treatment of personal revelation.

Not imported: continuing revelation beyond the canon. That is Layer 2, and Article 4 already holds the Restoration's position.

Apparatus instruction (Aaron, 22 Sep 2026): the Restoration column carries Moroni 10:5 (with 10:4 for context) as a pillar of the Restoration and of Latter-day Saint missionary work. The citation links to Volume 2 on personal revelation.

## R4. Witness of Christ (also covers item 19, by Aaron's ruling of 22 Sep 2026: no separate span): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026 (with 3 Nephi 11:32, 36 in the Restoration column)

> The Article confesses only what John 15:26 and 1 Corinthians 12:3 confess: the Spirit testifies of Christ, and no one can say that Jesus is Lord but by the Holy Spirit. This is the floor. It connects to the one boundary the Prologue names (AOID2-BOUNDARY). It does not settle how that witness is received or recognised.

Apparatus instruction (Aaron, 22 Sep 2026): the Restoration column carries 3 Nephi 11:32, 36, where Christ says the Holy Ghost bears record of the Father and of Him (pairs with John 15:26). The apparatus marks the cross-reference to the Prologue's boundary (1 Corinthians 12:3, AOID2-BOUNDARY) so the repetition reads as intended.

## R5. Sanctifier: RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026 (with 3 Nephi 27:20 in the Restoration column)

> The Article confesses only what 2 Thessalonians 2:13, 1 Peter 1:2 and Romans 15:16 confess: God sanctifies His people through the Spirit. This is the floor. It does not settle how sanctification relates to justification, whether it is completed in this life, or its connection to ordinances; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Not imported: any order of salvation (Reformed, Wesleyan, Orthodox theosis, or Restoration).

Apparatus instruction (Aaron, 22 Sep 2026): the Restoration column carries 3 Nephi 27:20, "that ye may be sanctified by the reception of the Holy Ghost."

## R6. Giver of gifts (item 13): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026 (all three Restoration anchors kept)

> The Article confesses only what 1 Corinthians 12:4-7, 11 confess: the Spirit gives diverse gifts to believers, as He wills, for the common good. This is the floor. It does not settle which gifts continue today, or how they are recognised or governed; each tradition fills that in its own house. The Restoration's fuller teaching (D&C 46; Moroni 10:8-18; Articles of Faith 1:7) is amplifying witness.

Not imported: continuationism or cessationism. Either one fails the floor for the other side.

Apparatus instruction (Aaron, 22 Sep 2026): the Restoration column carries D&C 46, Moroni 10:8-18 and Articles of Faith 1:7. Moroni 10:8-18 is marked as reading with R3's Moroni 10:4-5.
