## L1. The two great commandments (item 15): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with the Restoration anchors

> The Article confesses only what Matthew 22:36-40 confesses, quoting Deuteronomy 6:5 and Leviticus 19:18: the first and great commandment is to love the Lord our God with all our heart, soul and mind, the second is like unto it, to love our neighbor as ourselves, and on these two hang all the law and the prophets. This is the floor. The neighbor is not limited to those who share our faith (Luke 10:29-37). It does not settle how the two commandments order the rest of the law, or how love of God is expressed in worship; each tradition fills that in its own house.

Cross-reference: the Prologue's span (now A01 s19) uses the same passage as the key to reading "what Christ taught." There, Matthew 22:40 is the hermeneutic. Here it is the commandment itself. The apparatus marks the pairing so the repetition reads as intended.
Restoration column (ratified): D&C 59:5-6 (the two commandments restated in modern revelation); Moroni 7:45-48.

## L2. The Golden Rule (item 3): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with 3 Nephi 14:12

> The Article confesses only what Matthew 7:12 and Luke 6:31 confess: whatsoever we would that others should do to us, we do also to them, for this is the law and the prophets. This is the floor.

Restoration column (ratified): 3 Nephi 14:12.

## L3. Love of enemies (item 4): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with the Restoration anchors

> The Article confesses only what Matthew 5:44 and Romans 12:14, 17-21 confess: the disciple loves his enemies, blesses those who curse him, prays for those who persecute him, and overcomes evil with good. This is the floor. It does not settle whether a disciple may bear arms, the use of force by civil authority, or questions of self-defense; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Why the exclusion matters: the Anabaptist family holds nonresistance, and most others hold some form of just war. Importing either view fails the other.
Restoration column (ratified): 3 Nephi 12:44; D&C 98:16, 23-24.

## L4. Forgive as we are forgiven (item 14): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with D&C 64:9-10

> The Article confesses only what Matthew 6:12, 14-15 and Ephesians 4:32 confess: the disciple forgives others as God in Christ has forgiven him, and asks God's forgiveness as he forgives. This is the floor. It does not settle whether forgiveness waits on the offender's repentance, whether reconciliation or restored trust must follow, how the Church ministers God's forgiveness, or civil consequences; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Restoration column (ratified): D&C 64:9-10 ("of you it is required to forgive all men").

## L5. The hungry, the stranger, the sick and the imprisoned (item 2): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with the Restoration anchors

> The Article confesses only what Matthew 25:34-40 and Galatians 6:10 confess: the disciple feeds the hungry, welcomes the stranger, clothes the naked, and visits the sick and the imprisoned, and what is done for the least is done for Christ; we do good unto all, especially unto the household of faith. This is the floor. It does not settle how such works bear on final judgment or on justification; each tradition fills that in its own house.

Why the exclusion matters: faith-and-works is the most contested line in this Article. Galatians 6:10 carries "unto all" so the floor does not rest on the disputed reading of "my brethren" in Matthew 25:40, and so this Article is not scoped to believers, the gap the inventory found in Article 8.
Restoration column (ratified): Mosiah 2:17; Mosiah 4:26; D&C 52:40.

## L6. Hospitality (item 9, unscored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, kept separate from L5

> The Article confesses only what Romans 12:13, Hebrews 13:2 and 1 Peter 4:9 confess: the disciple is given to hospitality, without grudging, for some have entertained angels unawares. This is the floor.

Restoration column (ratified, Aaron's choice): Alma 8:19-21, with Alma 10:7-9 (Amulek receives the hungry stranger into his house, prepared by an angel; the pairing with Hebrews 13:2); D&C 124:22-24 (the Nauvoo House, "that strangers may come from afar to lodge therein"; the apparatus gives a line of context). Both are to be verified under the Source Verification Standard.

## L7. Honesty (item 8, unscored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, with plainer wording and Articles of Faith 1:13

> The Article confesses only what Exodus 20:16 and Ephesians 4:25 confess: the disciple puts away lying and speaks truth with his neighbor. This is the floor. It does not settle hard cases, such as deceiving to protect a life; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Restoration column (ratified): Articles of Faith 1:13 ("We believe in being honest, true…").

## L8. Pray for the sick (item 10, unscored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026, James quoted directly, with D&C 42:43-44

> The Article confesses only what James 5:14-16 confesses: the disciple prays for the sick, and "the prayer of faith shall save the sick, and the Lord shall raise him up." This is the floor. It does not settle whether anointing the sick is a sacrament or ordinance, who may administer it, or which gifts of healing continue today; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Why the exclusion matters: Catholic, Orthodox and Latter-day Saint readers hold anointing as a sacrament or priesthood ordinance; many others do not. The ordinance stays in the Restoration column (ratified: D&C 42:43-44). R6's continuation question does not re-enter here.

## L9. Honor rulers; render to Caesar (item 25, First-Generation Practice 11, optional, unscored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026; placed in ARTICLE 11, not the new Article 1

> The Article confesses only what Mark 12:17, Romans 13:1, 7 and 1 Peter 2:13-17 confess: disciples render to Caesar the things that are Caesar's and to God the things that are God's, give honor and what is due to those in authority, and pray for them; and where men's commands contradict God's, we ought to obey God rather than men (Acts 5:29). This is the floor. It does not settle when disobedience or resistance is right, oaths, military service, or the relation of church and state; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Why Acts 5:29 is inside the floor: every family holds both halves. Leaving the limit out would make the floor say more than any family teaches. Where the line falls is the dispute, and it stays out.
Placement (Aaron, 22 Sep 2026): Article 11, Of Covenants and Commitments, as part of the disciple's covenant to live in the world but not be of the world (John 17:14-16), beside s5's "in the world." All anchors kept. John 17:14-16 is the framing verse for the sentence; it is not part of this floor.
Restoration column (ratified): Articles of Faith 1:12 ("We believe in being subject to kings, presidents, rulers, and magistrates, in obeying, honoring, and sustaining the law"); D&C 134:1-5.

## L10. Work, and do not be a burden (item 26, First-Generation Practice 8, optional, unscored): RATIFIED by Aaron 22 Sep 2026; placed in ARTICLE 11, with the Restoration anchors

> The Article confesses only what 2 Thessalonians 3:10-12, 1 Thessalonians 4:11-12 and Ephesians 4:28 confess: disciples work with their own hands, eat their own bread, and labor that they may have something to give to him that needeth. This is the floor. It does not settle economic systems, public or church welfare, or the obligations of those who cannot work; each tradition fills those in its own house.

Why the last exclusion matters: the floor must not read as a judgment on the sick, the aged or the disabled. Ephesians 4:28 turns the teaching outward, toward giving, which ties it to L5.
Restoration column (ratified): D&C 42:42 ("Thou shalt not be idle"); D&C 75:28-29.
