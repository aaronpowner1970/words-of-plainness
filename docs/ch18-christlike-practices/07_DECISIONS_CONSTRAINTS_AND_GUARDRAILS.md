# Decisions, Constraints, and Guardrails

This file is the highest-authority design register in this handoff unless it conflicts with an established Words of Plainness project decision surfaced during Cowork reconciliation.

## A. Settled / strongly adopted decisions

1. **The material is best expressed as a reusable web application.**
2. **Replayability is central.** The app is somewhere the reader returns, not something the reader finishes.
3. **Practice cycles are the core formation unit.** A cycle may conclude; a Christlike practice does not become permanently completed.
4. **Eight-domain taxonomy is the current preferred architecture:**
   - Attentive Presence
   - Dignity & Belonging
   - Truth & Agency
   - Grace & Restoration
   - Humility & Inner Government
   - Peace & Right Use of Power
   - Servanthood & Stewardship
   - Forming Others
5. **The corpus target is approximately 70 observable practices.** The final number is not sacred.
6. **Stable CDP identifiers** independent of display order.
7. **Multiple discovery routes:** Help Me Choose, Choose a Domain, Life Situations, Browse All, For Today.
8. **Assessment preserves agency:** recommends, does not diagnose or assign.
9. **Behavior-first evidence model:** enacted evidence is distinguished from teaching-only and inference.
10. **Keep My Commandments remains conceptually distinct:** KMC = what Jesus commanded; Christlike Practices = what Jesus modeled.
11. **Preserve familiar WoP card grammar:** How We Practice / How It Blesses Lives / How Will You Practice?, with What Jesus Did added before them.

## B. Strong preferences that may be challenged with evidence

- Approximately 24 assessment items, initially three per domain.
- Recommend two domains rather than one.
- Four-point frequency scale plus Not applicable / unsure.
- Use “My Practice Journey” rather than “Progress.”
- Progressive disclosure rather than a 70-item landing wall.
- Life Situations as a major secondary navigation layer.

## D. Explicit non-goals

The product is **not**:

- a Bible trivia app;
- a theological orthodoxy test;
- a personality-type quiz;
- a spiritual-worth score;
- a 70-item completion checklist;
- a replacement for Chapters 19–29;
- a clone of Keep My Commandments;
- denominational commentary masquerading as textual observation;
- a gamified ladder of sanctification;
- a mechanism for telling users which virtue God “wants” them to work on;
- a substitute for professional psychological assessment or pastoral care.

## E. Language guardrails

Avoid:
- weakest virtue
- deficiency
- failed domain
- mastered
- completed virtue
- spiritual score
- diagnosis

Prefer:
- possible growth focus
- practice to consider
- explore this domain
- practice cycle
- reflect
- return
- practice again

## F. Methodological guardrails

1. Do not use creeds, denominational traditions, or commentary traditions as the primary reason a practice exists.
2. Prefer behaviors a disciple can meaningfully imitate or examine.
3. Do not inflate the corpus with synonyms merely to preserve a large number.
4. Flag textual-critical complications.
5. Avoid claiming that every modern application is the only meaning of a Gospel scene.

## G. UX guardrails

- Never require assessment before browsing.
- Never imply a recommendation is spiritually authoritative.
- Never require every practice in a domain before choosing one.
- Never punish inactivity or unfinished cycles.
- Never use streaks as the central formation mechanism.
- Never show “70/70 complete.”
- Make repeated practice easy and honorable.
- Make custom commitments first-class.
- Keep the Gospel behavior visible rather than burying it beneath generic self-help copy.
