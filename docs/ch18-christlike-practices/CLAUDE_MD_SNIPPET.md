## Chapter 18 — Christlike Practices App

The Chapter 18 companion app is a replayable discipleship-formation tool built around observable practices of Jesus in the New Testament.

Canonical product docs: `docs/ch18-christlike-practices/canonical/`

Core rule: the unit of content is a Christlike practice; the unit of user experience is a practice cycle. A cycle may close, but a practice is never permanently “completed” for a user.

Keep My Commandments and Christlike Practices are sibling products with distinct semantics:
- KMC: what Jesus commanded.
- Christlike Practices: what Jesus modeled.

Preserve stable `CDP-*` IDs. Assessment recommendations must preserve user agency and must not be presented as spiritual diagnosis or validated psychometrics unless separately validated.

Before changing schemas or shared UI, inspect the canonical Chapter 18 docs and existing shared Words of Plainness / Keep My Commandments conventions.
