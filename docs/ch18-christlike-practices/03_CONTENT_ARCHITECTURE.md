# Content Architecture

## Core conceptual model

> **Christlike Virtue Domain → Observable Discipleship Practice → Daily-Life Context**

The domains provide conceptual organization. The practices are the primary content objects. Life situations provide contextual discovery routes.

## Stable identifiers

Every practice should receive a stable identifier of the form `CDP-###`, where **CDP = Christlike Discipleship Practice**.

The identifier remains independent of display order or domain assignment.

The developmental registry currently runs `CDP-001` through `CDP-070`.

## Mature practice-card content model

Each practice should eventually contain:

1. Stable ID
2. Title
3. Short descriptor
4. Primary domain
5. Optional secondary domains
6. New Testament evidence
7. Evidence type
8. **What Jesus Did**
9. **How We Practice**
10. **How It Blesses Lives**
11. **How Will You Practice?**
12. Reflection prompts
13. Life Situation tags
14. Related practices
15. Textual-critical note, when needed
16. Editorial/source note, when needed

## Evidence discipline

- **Enacted:** narrative directly portrays Jesus performing the behavior.
- **Taught and enacted:** Jesus teaches the principle and narrative evidence portrays corresponding conduct.
- **Strongly inferred:** reasonable behavioral synthesis from narrative action but not explicitly named in the text.

Practices should not depend solely on a textually disputed passage where stronger corroborating evidence exists.

## Textual notes already identified

- John 7:53–8:11 should be marked for manuscript-history review. Compassion without permissiveness and deliberateness under pressure should not rely exclusively on this passage.
- Luke 23:34 should be treated cautiously because of early manuscript variation and should not be sole evidence for forgiveness/enemy-love.

## Cross-context architecture

A practice has one primary domain for navigation but may have secondary domains, many Life Situation tags, related-practice links, links to later Volume 1 chapters, and future Keep My Commandments links.
