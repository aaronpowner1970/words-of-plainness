# Approved Style Descriptors

Use these consistently across filenames, ID3 tags, YAML labels, and catalog entries.
When a style isn't listed, propose it to Aaron before using — consistency matters.

## Americana / Folk
*Core WoP sound — campfire authority, earthy warmth*

| Descriptor | Notes |
|-----------|-------|
| `Sacred_Americana` | Primary WoP identity |
| `Americana_Folk` | Broader folk umbrella |
| `Celtic_Ballad` | Irish/Scottish melodic influence |
| `Bluegrass` | Banjo/mandolin/fiddle-forward |

## Worship
*Active devotional register*

| Descriptor | Notes |
|-----------|-------|
| `Contemplative_Worship` | Quiet, meditative, spacious |
| `Soul_Worship` | Gospel-inflected, emotional intensity |
| `Celtic_Worship` | Irish melodic + devotional blend |
| `Acoustic_Worship` | Stripped-back guitar-led worship |

## Classical
*Orchestral / choral arrangements*

| Descriptor | Notes |
|-----------|-------|
| `Classical_Crossover` | Orchestral + contemporary blend |
| `Classical_Duet` | Two classical voices |
| `Choral_Anthem` | Full choir, Wilberg-style |

## Cinematic
*Dramatic / film-score quality*

| Descriptor | Notes |
|-----------|-------|
| `Cinematic_Inspirational` | Piano-led, orchestral build |
| `Atmospheric_Folk` | Ambient textures + folk bones |
| `Ethereal_Celtic` | Otherworldly Celtic treatment |

## Vocal Treatment
*Append to primary style when vocal treatment is distinctive*

| Descriptor | Notes |
|-----------|-------|
| `Female_Vocal` | Female lead vocal |

| `Duet` | Two distinct voices |

## Ambient / Instrumental
*Non-vocal pieces*

| Descriptor | Notes |
|-----------|-------|
| `Meditation` | Contemplative, spacious |
| `Instrumental` | General instrumental arrangement |
| `Soundscape` | Textural, atmospheric |

## Combination Rules

Combine by appending with underscore when needed:
- `Americana_Folk_Female_Vocal` — folk arrangement with female lead
- `Sacred_Harp_A_Cappella` — shape-note without instruments
- `Cinematic_Inspirational_Duet` — dramatic arrangement with two voices

Keep combinations to **three descriptors maximum** to avoid unwieldy filenames.

## Music Page Labels

For the website's "More Versions" panel and music page, labels can be:
- **Short:** "Celtic Worship" (2–3 words, self-explanatory styles)
- **Elaborated:** "Cinematic Inspirational — Piano-Led with Dramatic Orchestral Build"

Use em dash (—) to separate style name from brief elaboration.
Avoid generic labels like "Version 2" — give listeners a reason to click.
