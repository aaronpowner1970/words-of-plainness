## Overview

Words of Plainness is a two-volume interfaith Christian ministry website and book project. Volume 1 presents Christ-centered discipleship accessible to readers of any Christian background or spiritual condition. Volume 2 presents the covenants and systematic theology of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Both volumes live on the same website (commandments.brotheraaron.org) with separate landing pages, separate navigation menus, and self-contained reading experiences. A reader can complete Volume 1 without encountering Volume 2 content in their navigation.

### Guiding Principles

- **Interfaith accessibility** through Movements 1–3 and the Conclusion: no content requires prior Latter-day Saint theological commitments.
- **Biblical sources lead** the "Learn of Me" narrative; Restoration scriptures deepen and confirm as additional witnesses.
- **Implicit reader segmentation**: readers self-identify through engagement choices (commitment tiers in card-chapters) rather than explicit pathway selection.
- **Choose-your-own-adventure navigation**: readers may move between sections freely, returning to any content they initially skipped.

### Content Formats

- **Standard Prose Chapters**: traditional reading experience with reflection prompts (Movements 1, 2, Conclusion).
- **Interactive Card-Chapters**: three-tab adaptive format (How We Practice, How It Blesses, Your Commitment) with commitment tiers saving to the reader's "My Reflections & Goals" profile (Movement 3).

**Chapter 29 (Our Testimonies)** is Christ-centered and interfaith in character. The Volume 1 testimony says, "We know Christ lives." A reader from any Christian tradition (or none) should feel moved by this testimony even if they disagree with the authors' denomination.

**Chapter 30 (Conclusion & Benediction)** structure:
1. Review of the entire Volume 1 journey (finding faith → knowing Christ → practicing discipleship)
2. Encourage readers to revisit their reflections from "Learn of Me" chapters and commitment choices from card-chapters
3. Pastoral farewell
4. Invitation to stay connected through website features, discussion threads, and newsletter
5. Invitation to continue into Volume 2

## Phases of Development

### Phase 1 — Framework Launch (Immediate Priority)

**Objective:** Make the site presentable and navigable for VR sessions and early visitors. Communicate the full ministry vision to registered users and seekers.

**Tasks:**
- Build two-volume navigation structure with separate landing pages
- Write introduction/overview for each volume landing page
- Create placeholder pages for every chapter in both volumes (title, 2–3 sentence description, "Subscribe for notification" prompt)
- Retire deployed Prayer & Repentance chapter (redirect URL, remove from navigation)
- Author and deploy Chapter 6 postscript with self-assessment tool
- Update main Writings page to present two-volume architecture

**Deliverables:** Complete site framework with functional navigation for both volumes, live self-assessment tool at Chapter 6 branching point, placeholder pages for all 59 chapters.

### Phase 2 — Learn of Me (First Content Sprint)

**Objective:** Publish the Christological foundation that defines the ministry's identity.

**Build Order:**
1. **Chapter 9: Christ's Personal Character** — Centerpiece, most novel content. Draws from CCC presentation material, Beatitudes as character attributes, and manuscript "Law of the Gospel." Author knows this content deeply.
2. **Chapter 7: Prophecies, Birth, and Youth** — Drawn from Ms Ch 44–45 with biblical-first editorial discipline.
3. **Chapter 8: Baptism, Temptations, and Mortal Ministry** — Drawn from Ms Ch 46–47 with same approach.
4. **Chapter 10: Suffering, Trial, Crucifixion, and Resurrection** — Drawn from Ms Ch 48–50.
5. **Chapter 11: The Living Christ** — Brief transitional chapter. Written last because it summarizes and points forward.

**Deliverables:** Five complete "Learn of Me" chapters with reflection prompts, establishing the Christological foundation for the entire ministry.

### Phase 3 — Beatitudes and Sermon on the Mount (Bridge into Discipleship)

**Objective:** Build the first interactive card-chapters and test the full authoring pipeline.

**Build Order:**
1. **Chapter 12: The Beatitudes — Christlike Character**
2. **Chapter 13: The Sermon on the Mount — Christ's Standard for Discipleship**

**Deliverables:** Two complete card-chapters bridging "Learn of Me" into Discipleship Practices. Proven authoring pipeline (practice tabs, blessings tabs, commitment tiers, audio narration, musical testimony) for all subsequent card-chapters.

### Phase 4 — Character and Worship Card-Chapters (Core Practices)

**Objective:** Complete the first topical group of discipleship practices.

**Build Order:**
1. **Chapter 16: Keeping the Sabbath** — Smallest scope, single source, cleanest pipeline test after Phase 3.
2. **Chapter 14: Prayer as a Lifestyle** — Foundational practice; every subsequent chapter builds on this.
3. **Chapter 15: Repentance as a Lifestyle** — Foundational practice; includes Spiritual Cycles diagram.
4. **Chapter 17: Christlike Virtues** — Pairs naturally with Beatitudes (Ch 12) and Christ's Personal Character (Ch 9).

**Deliverables:** Complete Character and Worship group (6 card-chapters total including Phases 3–4), providing a substantial body of interactive discipleship content.

### Phase 5 — Remaining Card-Chapters (Batch Authoring)

**Objective:** Complete all 11 remaining card-chapters in Movement 3.

**Build Order by Group:**

*Relational Practices:* Ch 18 (Loving Our Neighbors) → Ch 19 (Building Godly Marriages) → Ch 20 (Raising Children in Righteousness)

*Stewardship Practices:* Ch 21 (Developing Our Talents) → Ch 22 (Financial Stewardship) → Ch 23 (Tending the Garden)

*Mission Practices:* Ch 24 (Witnessing of Christ) → Ch 25 (Faithful Service)

*Endurance Practices:* Ch 26 (Bearing Trials in Faith) → Ch 27 (Laying Up Treasures in Heaven) → Ch 28 (Enduring to the End)

Within each group, chapters with the most ready source material are authored first.

**Deliverables:** All 17 Movement 3 card-chapters complete and deployed.

### Phase 6 — Conclusion and Volume 2

**Objective:** Complete Volume 1 and begin Volume 2 authoring.

**Build Order:**
1. **Chapter 29: Our Testimonies** (interfaith, Christ-centered)
2. **Chapter 30: Conclusion & Benediction** — Written after full Volume 1 arc is complete so the journey review reflects actual published content.
3. Begin Volume 2 authoring, starting with chapters the author feels most prepared to write. The Volume 2 build order can be determined when Phase 6 begins.

**Deliverables:** Volume 1 complete. Volume 2 authoring underway.
